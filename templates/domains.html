Domains

Route by TLS SNI: each domain maps public ports to internal ip:port. Per port choose terminate (inproxy ends TLS with an automatic Let's Encrypt cert and reverse-proxies) or passthrough (the encrypted stream is forwarded as-is; the backend serves its own cert). A request that matches no domain falls through to normal handling (HTTP service / tools). HTTP is auto-redirected to HTTPS. Changes apply immediately.
